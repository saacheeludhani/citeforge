CiteForge — Research Notes

1. Project Overview

CiteForge aims to evaluate the reliability and consistency of information provided by AI-powered search and answer systems, with a focus on citations and source verification.

2. Problem Statement

AI-generated answers may contain unsupported claims, inaccurate citations, or sources that do not fully support the information presented. These issues can make it difficult for users to verify answers.

3. Key Areas of Research

- Answer Fidelity: Whether an answer accurately represents the information available in its cited sources.
- Citation Stability: Whether citations remain consistent and accessible across repeated checks.
- GEO Benchmarking: Evaluating how content is represented by generative engine systems.
- Self-Improvement: Using evaluation results to identify areas where an AI assistant can improve.

4. Integrity Auditing

The Integrity Auditor compares webpage responses obtained using different user-agent strings, extracts text and structured data, and checks robots.txt and sitemap availability.

5. Expected Impact

CiteForge aims to improve transparency, make citation verification easier, and help identify potential reliability issues in AI-generated answers.

6. Research Note

The project’s findings should be validated through testing. Differences in webpage responses do not automatically prove deliberate manipulation, and citation quality requires separate evaluation.
