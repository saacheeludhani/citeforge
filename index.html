    Is this a replacement for SEO?

    No. SEO helps people and systems discover your pages. CiteForge adds an evidence and testing layer: structuring trustworthy information and checking whether AI systems cite it accurately. Strong rankings don't guarantee inclusion in an AI answer.

    Can CiteForge guarantee that AI will cite my content?

    No. AI citations are unstable and can differ from run to run. CiteForge measures what happens and helps you improve the source.

    How is citation performance measured?

    We run generated questions through live-web AI search, record which sources are cited, and check claims against verified facts: citation rate, coverage and claim accuracy.

    Do I need to publish AI-generated content?

    No. The editor suggests changes for human review. Every claim should link to evidence you can verify.

    What happens after a test finds a problem?

    You see the gap, make a reviewed change, republish and retest on the same question set.
